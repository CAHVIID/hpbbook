Ceiling Fan Comfort

Adds a ceiling fan to an IDA ICE simulation, hour by hour. Load the TEMPERATURES.prn and IAQ.prn files of each zone, enter the fan datasheet, and the app finds when the fan runs, the SET and cooling effect, PMV by the ASHRAE 55 elevated air speed method, the cooling fan efficiency and the fan energy. Method as in chapter D6 and fan_comfort.py.
