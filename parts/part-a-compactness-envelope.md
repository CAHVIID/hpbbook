# About Part A

Part A covers building compactness and the building envelope: how the shape of a building and the quality of its envelope set the heat loss and the heat gains before any technical system is chosen.

- [The building envelope](../chapters/a1-envelope.md): heat loss through the envelope and thermal bridges, with the Thermal Bridge Lab app for 2D calculations.
- [Windows](../chapters/a2-windows.md): the window U-value, frames, edge seals and the energy balance of a window.
- [Glazing](../chapters/a3-glazing.md): U-value, g-value and light transmittance of glazing units.
